# Aura User Manual

## 1. Getting Started
1. Open the platform URL in your browser.
2. Register as a customer account or sign in with provided credentials.
3. Navigate to the storefront from the home page.

## 2. Customer Guide
### 2.1 Account and Profile
- Sign up with email and password
- Complete your fit profile for better VTO experience

### 2.2 Browsing and Product Selection
- Search or browse available categories
- Open product detail pages to review images, variants, and availability
- Select size/color variant before adding to cart

### 2.3 Virtual Try-On
1. Open a supported garment product page
2. Upload your photo
3. Submit VTO request
4. Wait for processing and review generated output
5. Save or continue to checkout

### 2.4 Cart, Checkout, and Orders
- Add selected items to cart
- Complete checkout details and payment
- Track order history and status in your account

## 3. Brand Owner Guide
### 3.1 Catalog Management
- Create and edit garments
- Add variants (size/color) and pricing
- Maintain product media

### 3.2 Inventory Operations
- Update stock per variant
- Monitor low-stock items and order impact

### 3.3 Order Management
- Review incoming orders
- Update fulfillment states
- Track completion and customer delivery lifecycle

### 3.4 Dashboard and Insights
- View order and try-on activity
- Use metrics to optimize catalog and conversion paths

## 4. System Administrator Guide
- Access admin-level controls for platform-wide management
- Monitor user/store health
- Apply governance and moderation actions

## 5. Troubleshooting
- **VTO not generating**: confirm external VTO token configuration
- **Payment issues**: verify billing provider keys and callback setup
- **Missing static/media files**: check deployment static collection and media paths
