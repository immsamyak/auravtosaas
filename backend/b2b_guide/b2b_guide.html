B2B Wholesale Complete Walkthrough

Here are the real, actual screenshots of how your wholesale integration works from end to end!

1

Configure Your Discount

Log into your Brand Owner dashboard at http://localhost:8000/login, go to Settings, and set your desired B2B Discount (e.g. 25%). Click Save.

2

Grant Wholesale Privileges

In your dashboard, navigate to the Customers tab. When a customer registers on your storefront, they appear here. Click the Grant Wholesale button next to their name.

3

The Customer Experience

When the customer logs in and visits your storefront (http://localhost:8000/store/alvicsx/), the system identifies them as a Wholesale Partner and automatically recalculates all prices site-wide!

In the screenshot below, notice how the retail price is crossed out, and the new 25% discounted B2B price is shown automatically!
